chi sono i nuovi europei? - big research on the new generations of Europeans

2024

a study developed by the younger generations and for the younger generations, to give voice to the key issues facing the present and future of the European Union. 9,943 responses, 15,000 people involved, 250,000 digital resources, 2,000 Italian municipalities. “Nuovi Europei” is not just a publication but a major research project.

research lead by scomodo, here.

graphic, animation design and voice: rebecca venzi

in collaboration with: Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung
