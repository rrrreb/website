balôn fanzine - piegarsi intorno a un giorno

2026

“balôn. piegarsi intono a un giorno” is a fanzine that tells the story of Turin’s historic flea market. it takes place on saturdays, from early morning until late at night, when borgo dora transforms into an imaginative village. almost every saturday from january through april, we met at Spazio Baom and tried to capture this experience in this publication.

project by: maria elena ferraresi and luca greco

coordinated by: giovanni cascella, alice de gregorio, elisa lanza of Fanzinne Magazine

edited by: Solo Cose Belle APS

texts by: lucrezia ceglie, camilla rondot, antonella quitadamo and viola bertolin

photos by: elena cecilia aragno and luca roggero

illustration by: margherita provera and maddalena luraghi

graphic design by: luca parri and rebecca venzi
